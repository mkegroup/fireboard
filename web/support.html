Support

Email hello@mkegroup.ca with your department name. We usually answer within one business day.

I didn't get the sign-in code

Check your junk or spam folder and search for Fire Ground Board. Codes can take a minute and work for 10 minutes. You can ask for a new one after 60 seconds.

How do I join my department?

Ask your chief for the invite link or 6-character code. Open the link, enter your email, type the code from the email, then your name.

I got a new phone

Install the app or open fire.mkegroup.ca and sign in with the same email. Everything is saved to your account, not your phone.

I'm not getting call alerts

In the app, open Me, On a call, and check that call alerts are on. Then check iPhone Settings, Fire Ground, Notifications. Alerts can be delayed by poor signal, so always rely on your department's pager or dispatch.

Make someone else the department owner

Department, Members, tap the person, Make owner.

Someone shouldn't have our invite code

Department, Make a new code. The old code stops working right away. People already in the department stay in.

Delete my account

In the app, open Me and tap Delete my account. Your login and profile are removed right away.

Plans and billing

The department owner manages the plan from the website, at fire.mkegroup.ca, under Department, Plan.
