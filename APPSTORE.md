## Description

Fire Ground Board puts your department's accountability board on every member's phone.

When you're paged, tap I'm responding and command sees you're coming. On scene, tap your name to change your status: Staged, In, Out, Rehab or Standby. Command sees it within seconds.

CALL PAR FROM YOUR PHONE
Call a PAR and every phone on the incident gets the alert. Crew confirm with one tap. Command sees who has answered and who hasn't, with a running clock.

MISSING ALARMS EVERY PHONE
Mark someone Missing and every device on the incident sounds an alarm, with their name on screen.

CALL ALERTS
Get alerted for new calls, PAR checks and Missing firefighters, even with the app closed.

BUILT FOR THE FIRE GROUND
- Big buttons and high contrast, readable in the dark or in sunlight
- Apparatus and mutual aid tracking
- Arrival report (IOCANC) builder
- Incident chat for things that don't need the radio
- Optional location sharing on an incident map
- Incident log and a downloadable report when the call is closed

RECORDS IN ONE PLACE
Roster, certifications with expiry warnings, training sessions and attendance, and equipment with service dates. Export any of it to a spreadsheet.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your department sees only its own data. Medical details and emergency contacts are visible only to the member and their officers.

Fire Ground Board supports your department's accountability system. It does not replace your radio, your PAR procedure or your standard operating guidelines.

Join with the invite code from your chief.

## App Privacy (the "nutrition label")

Tracking: **No**, the app does not track users.

Data collected, all **Linked to the user**, all for **App Functionality** only:

| Category | Data type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Info | Name | Shown to the department on the roster and board |
| Contact Info | Email Address | Sign-in |
| Contact Info | Phone Number | Optional, on the roster |
| Health & Fitness | Health | Optional medical notes, visible to the member and officers |
| Location | Precise Location | Only while the member shares it on an incident |
| User Content | Other User Content | Incident chat, notes and logs |
| Identifiers | User ID | Account |
| Identifiers | Device ID | Push alert token (OneSignal) |

Not collected: financial info, purchases, browsing history, search history, contacts, photos, audio, diagnostics, usage data for advertising.

## App Review information

Sign-in: check "Sign-in required" and enter the review account (see SETUP.md, "App Store review account").

Notes for the reviewer (paste this):

> Fire Ground Board is a crew accountability tool for fire departments. Departments are organizations. Individual firefighters are invited by their department with an invite code and never pay anything.
>
> To sign in with the review account: on the sign-in screen, tap "Password sign-in" at the bottom, then enter the email and password above. Normal users sign in with a one-time code sent to their email.
>
> The review account is the owner of "App Review FD", which has a sample incident. To try the main feature: open the incident, tap your name to change status, and tap Call PAR.
>
> You can also tap "Try the demo department" on the sign-in screen to see a department with live sample activity, no account needed.
>
> There is no purchasing in the app. Departments arrange service with MKE Group Inc. directly, outside the app.
>
> Location is only used when a member taps "Share my location" on an incident. Push notifications alert members to new calls, PAR checks and Missing firefighters.
