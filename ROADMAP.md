# Fire Ground Board: ideas and requests

Logged from conversations with fire service members. Build when it makes sense.

## From Andrew Curtis (acting captain, career + volunteer), Nov 26, 2026
He said the app is "80% there". The missing 20%:

1. **End-of-call report for OFM reporting.** When the call is over and crews are back at the station, the times, crew and actions are already logged. Turn that into the report departments file with the Ontario Fire Marshal.
2. **Full dispatch notes on the incident.** Bring the dispatch information in with the call, the way First Due does in Hamilton.
3. **Benchmark times.** Dispatch, en route, on scene, under control, back in service, captured as the call runs.
4. **Payroll-ready attendance.** Who attended which call and for how long, ready to hand to payroll.
5. **First Due integration.** Hamilton uses First Due (integrated with its dispatch), and Toronto is moving to it. Work alongside it, not against it.
6. **Back-office side for the chief.** A desktop view for records and reporting, separate from the fireground board.
7. **Security and hosting answers.** Where data lives, who can see it, what happens if someone hacks it. Answered at fire.mkegroup.ca/security.html.
8. **Outages.** What happens when service drops on scene.

His read on the market: start small (volunteer and composite departments), then move up. Municipalities and IT approval will be the main hurdle for bigger departments. Toronto and Hamilton are off limits for him (conflict of interest).
